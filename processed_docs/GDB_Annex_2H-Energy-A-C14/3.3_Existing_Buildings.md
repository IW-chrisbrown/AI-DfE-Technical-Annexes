## 3.3 Existing Buildings
3.3.1 The existing estate plays a critical role in delivering energy and carbon reduction
across the national school estate by 2050. [PM_40_20_26]
3.3.2 The extent of the Works will determine the level of intervention which can be made in
any Existing Building. [PM_40_20_26]
3.3.3 The Contractor shall maximise opportunity to reduce energy demand across Existing
Buildings where appropriate. [PM_40_20_26]
