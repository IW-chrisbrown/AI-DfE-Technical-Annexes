## 3.1 Design Benchmarks
3.1.1 During the design stage, the Contractor shall predict the total energy consumption of
the Building(s) that comprise the Works. [PM_40_30_27]
3.1.2 In order to monitor performance in use, 15 minute data to iSERV as described in the
section on In Use Monitoring shall be provided.  [PM_10_20_82]
3.1.3 The Contractor shall prove that the design can meet the energy targets through energy
modelling using the methodology defined in CIBSE TM54, as detailed in the section on
Energy Baseline Models.  [PM_40_30_27]
