## 4.3 Developed Energy Model
4.3.1 The Developed Energy Model for the Works shall be a realistic representation of
the final design of the Project. [PM_30_30_86]
4.3.2 The model shall accurately calculate the predicted regulated and unregulated
energy loads of the School. [PM_30_30_86]
4.3.3 The model and analysis of the results shall be included in the updated Energy
Strategy Report. [PM_40_20_26]
4.3.4 The model shall reflect the School-specific circumstances rather than the ‘fixed’ and
‘flexible’ input data required for the Concept Energy Model. [PM_30_30_86]
4.3.5 The anticipated annual energy end use figures shall be compared to the energy
targets as detailed in Energy and Net Zero Carbon. [PM_40_30_27]
4.3.6 The Contractor shall ensure that the model is consistent. [  PM_30_30_86]
4.3.7 Energy consumption estimates shall be included for all regulated and unregulated
loads in the model. [PM_30_30_86]
4.3.8 The model shall be designed to accurately predict the Building performance.
