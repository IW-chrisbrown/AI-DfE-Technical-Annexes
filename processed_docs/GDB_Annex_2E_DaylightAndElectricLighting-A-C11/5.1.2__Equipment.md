## 5.1.2  Equipment
5.1.2.1  Emergency battery packs shall be internal or external to luminaires. [Ss_70_80_33]
5.1.2.2  Central or distributed batteries shall be located in environments with ambient
temperature of 25
o
C or lower. [Ss_70_80_33]
5.1.2.3  Battery duration shall be at least 3 hours. [Ss_70_80_33]
5.1.2.4  The Contractor shall identify whether he is providing an emergency lighting
automatic testing and monitoring system. [PM_10_20_82]
5.1.2.5  Details shall be provided of the School’s responsibilities for testing the emergency
lighting system aligned with the emergency lighting risk assessment, Fire Regulation Reform
Order 2005, BS5266 pt1 and BS EN 50172. The details shall be included   in the O&M
Manuals. [PM_10_20_90]
5.1.2.6  The emergency lighting, testing and monitoring system shall consist of one of the
following options.
a)  Manual key-switches for each individual lighting final sub-circuit.
[Pr_70_70_47_02]
b)  A proprietary luminaire self-test system based upon individual luminaires.
[Pr_70_70_47_02]
c) A centralised dedicated proprietary emergency lighting test and monitoring
system. [Pr_70_70_47_02]
d)  Emergency lighting test and monitoring control algorithms as part of a centralised
lighting control system. [Pr_70_70_47_02]
