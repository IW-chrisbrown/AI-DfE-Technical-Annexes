## 3.2.2  Blinds
3.2.2.1  Blinds or other means of solar glare and daylight control shall be provided to all
exterior glazing (including rooflights) in Basic Teaching Areas,  learning resource areas and
staff areas (L1, L2 & L3), except within 15 degrees of absolute North where the Contractor
proves the visual environment is adequate without blinds. [Pr_30_59_07]
3.2.2.2  The type of blind(s) or shading employed shall be dependent on factors such as
window orientation, daylight performance and visual environment requirements of each
space. [Pr_30_59_07]
