4.4.   Commissioning and Building Performance Evaluation
4.4.1 The controls systems shall be fully commissioned throughout prior to handover.
[Ac_75_65_15]
4.4.2 The building services systems shall be commissioned such that where control
systems interact with each other they are commissioned at the same time in order to
simulate this interaction as accurately as possible. [Ac_75_65_15]
4.4.3 The Building Services control systems shall be commissioned in accordance with
CIBSE Commissioning Code C: Automatic controls (refer to Technical Annex 2F for details
of seasonal commissioning required). [  PM_10_20_90]
4.4.4 A commissioning programme shall be provided prior to construction. The programme
shall capture a two week notice period prior to witness testing. [PM_60_30_13]
4.4.5 Inspection and testing shall be undertaken in line with BS 7671 ‘Requirements for
electrical installations. IET Wiring Regulations’. [PM_10_20_90]
4.4.6 The controls system shall be commissioned in accordance with BSRIA AG 9/2001
‘Standard specifications for BMS’. [  PM_10_20_90]
