## 2.4.1  General Requirements
2.4.1.1 The School shall be zoned appropriately to ensure that:
a)  spaces are flexible in use [PM_10_20_82]
b)  spaces can be used in isolation out of hours where required [PM_10_20_82]
c)  services shall be capable of being controlled to account for differing weather/solar
gain characteristics. [PM_10_20_82]
2.4.1.2 As a minimum, the heating and cooling shall be zoned so that the following are
provided with separate zones:
a)  Sports facilities, including change areas and toilets. [PM_10_20_82]
b)  Main hall, including any catering facilities, and toilets and connecting corridors.
