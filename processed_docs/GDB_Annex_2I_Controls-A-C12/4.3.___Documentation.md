## 4.3.   Documentation
4.3.1 A detailed description of the controls systems and operating instructions shall be
included in the operation and maintenance manuals. [PM_70_85_13]
4.3.2 All testing and commissioning certificates shall be submitted prior to Practical
Completion and that the certificates include the test equipment identity and certificates of
calibration.  [PM_70_15]
4.3.3 A hard copy and an electronic copy of the testing and commissioning certificates of
the controls systems shall be submitted with each copy of the Operating and Maintenance
Manuals and ‘As Installed’ (record) drawings.  [PM_70_15]
