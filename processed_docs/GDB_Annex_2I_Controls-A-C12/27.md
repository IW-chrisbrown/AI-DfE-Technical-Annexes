3.5.5 Emergency push buttons shall be provided in any laboratories adjacent to the
teacher’s desk, in prep rooms and by the exit so that when pressed the gas valve shall
close. [Ss_75_50_33]
3.5.6 Where gas is supplied to teaching areas such as science laboratories, science prep
room, food technology classrooms and vocational training catering kitchen, a gas
isolation/pressure proving system where required by IGEM UP/11 shall be fitted.
