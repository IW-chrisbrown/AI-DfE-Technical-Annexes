5.1 General requirements
5.1.1 The following reference standards have been provided to support the development
and integration of the new requirements. [PM_10_20_90]

calibration for evaluation of energy in use [FI_70_85]
c) CIBSE TM65: Embodied Carbon in Building Services (2021) [FI_70_85]
d)  CIBSE TM54: Evaluating the operational performance of buildings at design stage
