## 12.1 Overview
12.1.1 Take account of the following reference standards (or updated documents if
relevant) or guidance:
d)  DD171:1987 -  ‘Guide to specifying performance requirements for hinged or
pivoted doors (including test methods)’ [FI_70]
e)  BS EN ISO 717 - 1:2013 -  ‘Acoustics. Rating of sound insulation in buildings and
of building elements’ [FI_70_85]
f)   BS EN 12400:2002 - ‘Windows and pedestrian doors. Mechanical durability.
Requirements and classification’ [FI_70_85]
g)  BS EN 1192:2000 - ‘Doors. Classification of strength requirements’ [FI_70_85]
h)  BS EN 1634-3:2004 -  ‘Fire resistance and smoke control tests for door and shutter
assemblies, openable windows and elements of building hardware. Smoke control
test for door and shutter assemblies’ [FI_70_85]
i)   BS EN 1294:2000 - ‘Door leaves. Determination of the behaviour under humidity
variations in successive uniform climates’ [FI_70_85]
j)   BS EN 60839-11-1:2013 -  ‘Alarm systems. Access control systems for use in
security applications. System requirements’ [FI_70_85]
k) BS EN 10874:2012 - ‘Resilient, textile and laminate floor coverings.  Classification’.
