## 5.6 Pull Handles
5.6.1    Pull handles on doors shall meet the requirements of BS 8424 - ‘Building hardware.
Pull handles. Requirements and test methods’. [Pr_30_36_59_64]
5.6.2    Pull handles shall:
a)  have a diameter of between 19mm and 35mm [Pr_30_36_59_64]
b)  be offset from the door leaf by at least 45mm, be at least 400mm long
[Pr_30_36_59_64]
c) contrast with the door leaf by a minimum of 20 LRV points [Pr_30_36_59_64]
d)  be fixed so the bottom end is no lower than 700mm and no higher than 1000mm
above the floor and the top no lower than 1300 above the floor [Pr_30_36_59_64]
e)  have bolt-through fixings. [Pr_30_36_59_64]
5.6.3 Pull type handles shall not be fitted to the push side of doors. [PM_10_20_82]
