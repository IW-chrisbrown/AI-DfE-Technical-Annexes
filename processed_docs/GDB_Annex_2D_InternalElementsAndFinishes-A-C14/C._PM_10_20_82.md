## C. [PM_10_20_82]
9.3.11 The temperature and humidity level of the substrate shall be tested before paint
application and the results recorded. [PM_10_20_82]
9.3.12 Painting shall not take place if the substrate temperature or humidity level is below
the manufacturer’s recommendations. [PM_10_20_82]
9.3.13 Sample rooms shall contain benchmark paint finishes and shall be approved by
the manufacturer and the Employer. [PM_10_20_82]
