## 5.8 Finger Guards
5.8.1 Finger guards shall be fitted to all doors to all spaces used by Early Years and Key
Stage 1 pupils, and in any space identified for SEN, where it is very likely for pupils to be
present (i.e., spaces that are categorised as Basic Teaching, Learning Resources or Halls,
Dining and PE as defined in BB103 or BB104). [Pr_30_36_36_04]
5.8.2 Finger guards shall be provided in non-teaching spaces if required in the SSB.
[Pr_30_36_36_04]
5.8.3 Where new doors require finger guards, the guard mechanism shall be either built into
the door and frame, with a rounded stile, or surface fixed to the door face and frame. In both
cases they shall comply with Classification Class 2 as defined in Table 1, BS 8613:2017 -
‘Finger protection devices for pedestrian doors’. [Pr_30_36_36_04]
5.8.4 Where retained or refurbished doors and frames require finger guards, an applied
cover finger guard shall comply with Classification Class 2 as defined in Table 1, BS
8613:2017 – ‘Finger protection devices for pedestrian doors – Specification’.
[Pr_30_36_36_04]
5.8.5 The cover finger guard shall not affect the integrity of the doorset in terms of fire or
acoustics. [Pr_30_36_36_04]
5.8.6 The proposed solution shall require minimal maintenance. [Pr_30_36_36_04]
5.8.7 See GDB Internal Elements and Finishes, Table 6   for Minimum Life Expectancy.
