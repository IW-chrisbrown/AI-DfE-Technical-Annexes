## 12
4.1.5 Performance test certificates shall be provided for all fire-rated doorsets -  including
their ironmongery and any fingerguards - in accordance with British or European Standards,
including BS 8214 -  ‘Code of practice for fire door assemblies’. [PM_10_20_90]
4.1.6 Acoustically rated doorsets shall conform to BB93. [Ss_25_30_20_25]
4.1.7 Where the need is identified within the SSB, a stable-type door may be used to provide
access to administration offices or a technicians’ area as per the fire strategy e.g.,   where
space for a second internal reception desk to the main office is constrained, or where pupil
access to a technician’s space is preferred. [Ss_25_30_20_25]
4.1.8 Stable doors shall be based on doorset type D1.1.a in Table 2. [Ss_25_30_20_25]
4.1.9 All doors shall have:
a)  a minimum clear opening width of 825mm through one leaf [Ss_25_30_20_25]
b)  f  ire and smoke resistance dependent on fire strategy [Ss_25_30_20_25]
c)  acoustic properties dependent on the acoustic strategy. [Ss_25_30_20_25]
