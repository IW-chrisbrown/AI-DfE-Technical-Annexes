## 5.5 Lever Handles
5.5.1 Lever handles on any doors shall meet the requirements of BS EN 1906 - ‘Building
hardware. Lever handles and knob furniture. Requirements and test methods’ and shall be
compatible with the locks with which they are to be used. [Pr_30_36_59_25]
5.5.2 Lever handles shall:
a)  have a diameter of between 19mm and 35mm [Pr_30_36_59_25]
b)  be offset from the door leaf by at least 45mm [Pr_30_36_59_25]
c) be at least 95mm long [Pr_30_36_59_25]
d)  have a return end, and contrast with the door leaf by a minimum of 20 LRV points
[Pr_30_36_59_25]
e)  shall have bolt-through fixings.  [Pr_30_36_59_25]
