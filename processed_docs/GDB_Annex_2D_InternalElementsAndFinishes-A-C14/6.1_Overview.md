## 6.1 Overview
6.1.1 All guarding provided shall meet the following requirements in addition to AD K and BS
6180:2011 -  ‘Barriers in and about buildings’:
a)  guarding of walkways or staircases with voids on both sides shall be 1250mm high
[Ss_25_60_05_05]
b)  any occupied, furnished mezzanine area (such as a learning resource area) shall
have guarding 1500mm high adjacent to the void [Ss_25_60_05_05]
c)  any additional requirements for the height of guarding based on a risk assessment
and specified in the SSB shall be met [PM_10_20_90]
d)  all safety glass shall meet the requirements of the section on Internal Stairs, Barriers,
Balustrades and Guarding and Section 8.4 on Barriers, Balustrades and Guarding in
Technical Annex 2B [ PM_10_20_90]
e)  t  he design of stairs and handrails shall be coordinated so that handrails are
continuous without vertical steps in the handrails. [Pr_25_30_36]
