## 5.4 Electro-magnetic Hold-open Devices
5.4.1 Electro-magnetic hold-open devices shall not be fitted to doorsets required to be self-
closing in order to provide privacy, such as doorsets to changing rooms. [PM_10_20_82]
5.4.2 Electro-magnetic devices shall be provided on self-closing fire doors in halls and
corridors. These shall release automatically as required by the fire strategy.
[Pr_75_30_23_27]
5.4.3 Appropriate measures shall be included to prevent accidental impact with the leading
edge of the door leaf, when in the open position. [PM_10_20_82]
