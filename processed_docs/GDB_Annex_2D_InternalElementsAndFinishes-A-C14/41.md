## 41
8   Ceilings and Soffits
8.1 Ceilings and Soffits Performance
8.1.1 Ceilings and soffits shall be smooth and free from holes. [Ss_30_47]
8.1.2 Service penetrations shall be neat and tidy. [Ss_30_47]
8.1.3 Ceilings or soffits shall conform to the specifications indicated in Table 7   for each
space, to match the relevant code in Technical Annex 1A or 1B,  or the School-specific ADS.
