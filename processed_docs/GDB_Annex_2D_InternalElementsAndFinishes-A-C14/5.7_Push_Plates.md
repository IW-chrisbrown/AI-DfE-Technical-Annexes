## 5.7 Push Plates
5.7.1 Push plates shall be provided to each door leaf on the face used to push the door
open. [Pr_30_36_33_68]
5.7.2 Push plates shall be a minimum of 90mm x 500mm in size. [Pr_30_36_33_68]
5.7.3 If a single push plate is not compatible with other hardware fitted to the door, two
minimum 90mm width plates fitted above and below the lock position and shall be provided
to protect to an equivalent extent. [Pr_30_36_33_68]
