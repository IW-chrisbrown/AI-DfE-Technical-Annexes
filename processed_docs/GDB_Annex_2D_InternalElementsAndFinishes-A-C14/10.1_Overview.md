## 10.1 Overview
10.1.1 The main site entrance sign shall detail the name of the School and other
pertinent information. The sign shall be of a design that allows for the incorporation of
changes when necessary and gives a positive first impression. [EF_40_10]
10.1.2 External directional signage provided shall be consistent and clearly visible,
especially for visitors, giving clear directions. [EF_40_10]
10.1.3 The signage shall be capable of alteration where appropriate, whilst preventing
the opportunity for tampering. [EF_40_10]
10.1.4 External building signage to main entrances (either façade or canopy mounted)
shall be clearly visible. [EF_40_10]
10.1.5 Signage shall be provided for every room and space as detailed in the ADS,
denoting its name or purpose and its agreed number. [EF_40_10]
10.1.6 Signage shall identify suites (of spaces), such as Departments. [EF_40_10]
10.1.7 See the section on Internal Doorset Performance for requirements for the
identification of fire rated doorsets. [EF_40_10]
10.1.8 Sign surfaces shall be sufficiently hard to resist impact from hand-held objects
without any noticeable change to the surface appearance. [EF_40_10]
10.1.9 Surfaces shall resist abrasion from cleaning and maintenance regimes without any
noticeable change in surface appearance. [EF_40_10]
10.1.10 All fixings used shall be suitable for their intended purpose and shall be in
accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations. [EF_40_10]
