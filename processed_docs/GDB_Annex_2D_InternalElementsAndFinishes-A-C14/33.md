are both acceptable), with smooth welds in sheet materials. Those rated ‘high’ within
Table 6   shall have no dirt traps and shall incorporate coved skirting [Ss_30_42]
b)  smoothness -  with minimal abrasion characteristics against the skin [Ss_30_42]
c)  sound absorption and transmission - ensuring good acoustic properties and
performance; floor finishes shall be as a whole alongside other internal surfaces to
achieve the performance criteria specified in BB93 [Ss_30_42]
d)  i  mpact resistance - shall resist indentations and marks and be co-ordinated with

e)  colour and pattern -  in terms of maintenance, wayfinding and in Special Schools,
sensitivity [Ss_30_42]
f)   suitability for Mobility Equipment users and others with a physical disability or sensory
impairment [Ss_30_42]
g)  having a low Volatile Organic Compounds (VOC) finish. [Ss_30_42]
