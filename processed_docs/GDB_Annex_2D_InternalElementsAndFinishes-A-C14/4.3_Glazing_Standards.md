## 4.3 Glazing Standards
4.3.1 All glazing shall meet the following requirements:
a)  all glazing shall comply with BS 6262: Part 1:2017 General methodology for the
selection of glazing [Ss_25_60_35]
b)  all safety glass in critical locations (defined in Section 5 of AD K4 Protection against
impact with glazing) shall be third party certificated and marked in accordance with
BS 6262-4:2005 - ‘   Glazing for Buildings’  [Ss_25_60_35]
