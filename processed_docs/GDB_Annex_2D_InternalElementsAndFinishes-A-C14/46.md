9.1.18 Where waterborne paints are used within reach of pupils there shall be a minimum
requirement for the wall paints to gain a Class 1 classification using the BS EN ISO 11998 -
'Paints and varnishes. Determination of wet-scrub resistance and cleanability of coatings'
test method or a Class C classification using the BS 7719 - 'Specification for waterborne
emulsion paints for interior use' test method. [Ss_40_90_60_95]
9.1.19 Normal duty finishes (i.e.,  those not classed as ‘heavy duty’) shall be finished in a
minimum of a trade high performance matt acrylic paint. [Ss_40_90_60]
9.1.20 All colours shall be agreed with the School. [Ss_40_90_60]
9.1.21 Brilliant white shall be avoided in circulation areas including stairs and Replaced with
either an off-white, or a colour agreed with the School. [Ss_40_90_60]
9.1.22 Kitchens and food rooms shall be finished in high performance eggshell antibacterial
paint. [Ss_40_90_60]
9.1.23 Areas requiring frequent cleaning in kitchen food preparation areas shall be finished
in smooth white uPVC, stainless steel or similar approved easy clean sheet materials.
[Ss_25_20]
9.1.24 Where Table 8 identifies the hygrothermal performance as humid or wet, mould
resistant finishes shall be used. [PM_10_20_90]
9.1.25 No products shall be used that require excess paint or solvent used in cleaning to be
disposed of at a hazardous waste site. [PM_10_20_82]
9.1.26 All empty cans shall be recycled using the appropriate recycling scheme through the
applicable paint merchant. [PM_10_20_82]

detergents, disinfectants and other liquids likely to be encountered in school buildings
