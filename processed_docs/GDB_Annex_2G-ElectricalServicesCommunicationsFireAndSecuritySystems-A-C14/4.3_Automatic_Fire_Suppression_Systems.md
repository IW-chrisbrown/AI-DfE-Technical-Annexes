## 4.3 Automatic Fire Suppression Systems
4.3.1 Where a sprinkler system is to be provided, it shall be designed, installed and
maintained in accordance with BS EN 12845. In BS EN 12845 schools are generally
classified as Ordinary Hazard Group 1 (OH1). [PM_10_20_82]
