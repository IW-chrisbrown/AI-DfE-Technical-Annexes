## [PM_35_10_25]
2.3.3.20 The switch rooms prevent the ingress of water and underground cable entries
are watertight. [PM_35_10_96]
2.3.3.21 The switchgear shall not be located below wet services such as water tanks,
drainage, piped water services, toilets, and plant rooms containing wet services, and not
within basement or rooms prone to flooding. [PM_35_10_96]
2.3.3.22 The layout of switch rooms shall allow for adequate working space. In the case
of withdrawable switchgear, working space is allowed to the front of the switchgear whilst
in the withdrawn position. The switch room allows for expansion of switchgear.
