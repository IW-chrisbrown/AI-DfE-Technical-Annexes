## [SL_25_10_61]
4.1.5 Outdoor Class Spaces provided outside a Special School or AP classroom shall be
a minimum of 30m2 per classroom, and shall be provided with an external canopy of
15m2 per classroom and be at least 2.4m wide. [SL_25_10_61]
4.1.6 The canopy provided to the Outdoor Class Space outside an Early Years or Special
School or AP classroom shall:
a)  have a structure that is robust and corrosion resistant, and able to resist dead,
live, wind and snow loads relevant to its location [Ss_25_50_45_10]
b)  be adjacent to the classroom and designed so as not to obstruct the Building’s
method of ventilation and daylight design [Ss_25_50_45_10]
