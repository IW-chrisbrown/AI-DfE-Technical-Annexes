## 3.5 Roof Access
3.5.1 Access shall be designed to meet the safety requirements of workplace, health and
safety legislation including, but not limited to, The Construction (Design and
Management) Regulations 2015, The Workplace Health, Safety and Welfare Regulations
1992, and The Work at Height Regulations 2005. [PM_10_20_90]
3.5.2 Access provided shall ensure safety during the life of the Building by:
a)  ensuring that the Maintenance Access Strategy, wherever practicable, locates
plant and equipment to eliminate the need for access via roof areas
