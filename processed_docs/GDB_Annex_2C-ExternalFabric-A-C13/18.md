3.9 Drainage and Rainwater Disposal Installations
3.9.1 Rainwater disposal installations shall meet the Minimum Life Expectancy
requirements in Table 5 in the GDB. [PM_35_10_47]
3.9.2 Roof drainage shall be designed in accordance with Technical Annex 2F and shall
have a simple layout, with free flowing, short and direct routes fully accessible for
maintenance. Drainage shall not have traps with internal outlets. [PM_10_20_90]
3.9.3 The layout shall be coordinated with the layout of all parts of the external walls.
