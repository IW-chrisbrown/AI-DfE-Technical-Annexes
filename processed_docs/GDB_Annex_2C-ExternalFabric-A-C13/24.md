## 24
5 External Doors and Windows
5.1 External Doors and Windows - General Requirements
5.1.1 External doors and windows shall meet the Minimum Life Expectancy requirements
in Table 5 in the GDB. They shall be designed to accommodate wind pressure to suit
location, height above sea level, exposure and Building configuration. Framing shall be
thermally broken and self-draining. Composite assemblies of windows with lateral joints
instead of curtain walling are not permitted. Frame fixings shall suit Pascal rating and
avoid cold bridging. [PM_35_10_47]
5.1.2 External doors and associated hardware and mechanisms shall be designed in
coordination with the Access and Security Strategy and the Fire Strategy, taking account
of the School’s existing safeguarding policy including the control of emergency exits.
