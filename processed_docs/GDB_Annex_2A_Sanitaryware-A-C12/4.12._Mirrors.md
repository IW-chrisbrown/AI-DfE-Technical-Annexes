## 4.12. Mirrors
4.12.1  Mirrors provided shall:
a)  be toughened glass or an alternative material.  [Pr_25_71_53]
b)  be backed with an impact-resistant safety layer to allow them to be fixed directly
to the timber framework behind. [Pr_25_71_53]
c) have tamper-proof fixings, preferably with adhesive. [Pr_25_71_53]
d)  have no sharp edges or corners.  [Pr_25_71_53]
