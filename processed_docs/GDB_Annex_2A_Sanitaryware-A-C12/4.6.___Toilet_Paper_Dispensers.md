## 4.6.   Toilet Paper Dispensers
4.6.1 Toilet paper dispensers shall be provided for every toilet pan. [Pr_40_70_22_90]
4.6.2 Toilet paper dispensers shall be a ‘sheet type’ dispenser rather than rolls.
[Pr_40_70_22_90]
4.6.3 Toilet paper dispensers shall be fitted with a tamper-proof lock to prevent pupil
interference. [Pr_40_70_22_90]
4.6.4 Where the SSB identifies that the School wish to provide their own dispensers
through a service contract, the Contractor shall allow for fixing only. [Pr_40_70_22_90]
