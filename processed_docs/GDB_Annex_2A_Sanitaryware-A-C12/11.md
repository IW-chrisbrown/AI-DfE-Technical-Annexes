3.2.2    Cubicle doors shall be lockable and opened manually from the inside.
[Ss_25_12_60_60]
3.2.3    Inward opening doors shall be able to open outwards through use of an emergency
releasable door hinge or a lift-off facility to facilitate emergency assistance to someone who
has fallen against the door inside the cubicle (blocking the door from opening).
[Ss_25_12_60_60]
3.2.4 An emergency release function shall limit operation to School staff only, thereby not
compromising the privacy of the user under general circumstances. [Ss_25_12_60_60]
3.2.5    Cubicle doors shall be fitted with occupation indicator signs,  using the word
'occupied’,  and shall be clearly visible from the outside of the cubicle door. A red/green
indicator is unsuitable. [Ss_25_12_60_60]
3.2.6 Cubicle locking devices shall be suitable for use by people with impaired manual
dexterity (i.e.,  a simple closed fist action). [Ss_25_12_60_60]
3.2.7 Cubicle locks shall be designed so that it is the weakest element in the door
assembly. The lock shall break on application of force rather than damaging the cubicle
system. [Pr_30_36_08_26]
3.2.8 The School shall be provided with spare locks to help reduce the time that cubicles
are out of action. [Pr_30_36_08_26]
3.2.9 The inside of each cubicle door shall have a coat hook with a rubber buffer, designed
to serve as a doorstop to the cubicle door. [Pr_30_36_59_38]
3.2.10 The coat hook shall be securely fixed with hidden fixings to avoid damage.
[Pr_30_36_59_38]
3.2.11 All hinges shall be designed to eliminate the potential for finger trapping and shall be
