4.2.   Toilet Pans and Cisterns
4.2.1    Toilet pans and exposed cisterns shall be constructed from vitreous china as set out
in BS 3402 - ‘Specification for quality of vitreous china sanitary appliances’ or a material
which can be demonstrated to perform equally well. [PM_10_20_90]
4.2.2 All parts of the toilet construction shall be sourced from a single manufacturer.
