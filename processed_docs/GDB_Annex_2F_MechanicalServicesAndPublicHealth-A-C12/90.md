8.3.   Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF) and Split Systems
8.3.1    Where VRF or split systems are used, the following requirements shall be met.
a)  Pipe lengths shall be minimised to reduce the volume of refrigerant.  [Ss_65_80]
b)  Leak detection shall be installed to minimise refrigerant losses and protect occupant
safety.  [Ss_65_80]
c) Maximum total pipe lengths from outdoor to indoor units, furthest indoor unit from the
outdoor unit, maximum height difference between outdoor unit and lowest indoor unit
shall all be within the manufacturer’s recommended maximum lengths [Ss_65_80]
d)  The equipment shall be easily accessible for maintenance. [PM_80_10_50]
