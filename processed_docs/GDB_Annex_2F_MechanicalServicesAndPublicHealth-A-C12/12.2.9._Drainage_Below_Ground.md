## 12.2.9. Drainage Below Ground
12.2.9.1 All necessary below-ground foul and surface drainage systems required to
drain all the Buildings’ soil vent pipes, stub stacks and rainwater pipes shall be designed,
supplied, installed, tested and commissioned. [Ss_50_35_08]
12.2.9.2 Manholes shall not be located in areas likely to be used as pitches.
