## 7.4.2. General Requirements
7.4.2.1 Typical server rooms can produce high-density heat loads which can affect ICT
equipment. The environmental conditions shall be maintained in line with the manufacturer’s
recommendations for the equipment to be housed within, or any respective warranty
requirements. [PM_35_70_94]
7.4.2.2 Security of ventilation openings in the façade shall comply with the GDB.
