## 11.4. Mechanical Ventilation
11.4.1 Where hybrid ventilation is being proposed, the mechanical ventilation element
needs to be modelled correctly. [PM_35_70_94]
11.4.2 If it is supply and extract ventilation, then a fixed or demand-controlled ventilation
rate of outside air shall be incorporated in the model. [ PM_35_70_94]
11.4.3 If the system is extract only with openable windows, the model shall be set up with a
zone exhaust and not an exchange rate to outside. [PM_35_70_94]
11.4.4 Note: For thermal modelling and overheating assessment purposes, mechanical
ventilation is classified as ‘free- running’ in the absence of mechanical cooling and tight
temperature control. [ PM_35_70_94]
