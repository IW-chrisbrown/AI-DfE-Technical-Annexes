10.5.6 The design shall allow air movement to be increased during the summer through

thermal simulation and CIBSE weather files in accordance with the requirements on weather
files given in Section 11.5.1. [PM_35_10_13]

occupied spaces and there shall be opening windows or vents with sufficient effective
opening area. [ PM_40_20_94]
