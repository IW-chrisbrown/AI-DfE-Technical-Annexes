## 6.3.   Local Extract Ventilation
6.3.1 This section gives the requirements for local extract ventilation e.g.,  from toilets,
washrooms, photocopiers and printers. [Ss_65_40_33_48]
Room  Local extract

Rooms containing printers
and photocopiers in
substantial use (greater
than 30 minutes per hour).
Air extract rate of 20 l/s per machine during use is
required to eliminate pollutants. Air must be
exhausted outside the Building. If located in a
separate room the room must be ventilated at the
rate of 10 l/s/person when occupied as for an
office. These rates are those given in AD F.
