## Category  Explanation

I - Equivalent to Category A
of BS EN ISO 7730: 2005
High level of expectation and also
recommended for spaces occupied by
very sensitive and fragile persons with
special requirements like some disabilities,
sick, very young children and elderly
persons to increase accessibility
II - Equivalent to Category B
of BS EN ISO 7730: 2005

Normal expectation
III - Equivalent to Category C
of BS EN ISO 7730: 2005

An acceptable moderate level of
expectation
