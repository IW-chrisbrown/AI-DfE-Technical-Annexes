## Components
3.9.2.1 The following requirements shall be met for any domestic hot and cold water system
valves, fittings and components.
a)  Pressure gauges shall be installed in line with BS EN 837-1 and temperature gauges
installed in line with BS EN 13190. [PM_10_20_90]
b)  WRAS or Kiwa KUKreg4 approved pipeline strainers shall be installed.
