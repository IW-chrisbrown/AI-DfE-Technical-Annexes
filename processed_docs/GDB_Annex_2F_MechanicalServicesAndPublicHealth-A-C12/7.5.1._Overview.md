## 7.5.1. Overview
7.5.1.1 This section includes the requirements for the design and installation of the
ventilation and associated systems in workshops including fumes from heat bays and wood
dust extract where provided. [PM_35_70_94]
7.5.1.2 Ventilation for DT studios, workshops and DT prep rooms shall provide controllable
systems designed for dust and fumes that may be produced. [PM_35_70_94]
