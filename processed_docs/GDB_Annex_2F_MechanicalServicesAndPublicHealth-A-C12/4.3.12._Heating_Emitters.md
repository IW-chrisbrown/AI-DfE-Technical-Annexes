## 4.3.12. Heating Emitters
4.3.12.1 Any space or area can be heated by one or more of the following emitter
types:
a)     Natural convectors and radiators.  [Pr_70_60_36_73]
b)     Warm air.  [Pr_70_60_36_02]
c)   Radiant heating. [Pr_70_60_36_71]
d)     Underfloor heating. [Pr_70_60_36_49]
e)     Forced convection fans.  [Pr_70_60_36_30]
4.3.12.2 Detailed reasons and justification shall be provided when proposing an
alternative form of heat emitter. [PM_10_20_82]
4.3.12.3 Heat emitters shall be selected based on the following requirements:
a)  Heating capacity required. [Pr_70_60_36]
b)  The responsiveness required due to change of use in the space. [Pr_70_60_36]
c) Thermal comfort requirements, see Section 10. [Pr_70_60_36]
d)  Space available (whether space is available on the floor, around the perimeter or at
high level).  [Pr_70_60_36]
e)  Safety requirements (including low surface temperature).  [Pr_70_60_36]
f)   Robustness.  [Pr_70_60_36]
g)  Running cost.  [Pr_70_60_36]
