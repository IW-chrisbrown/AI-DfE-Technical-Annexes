## 4.4.14. Fans
4.4.14.1 Where a mechanical or mixed-mode system is installed, the options to provide
ventilation through fans include:
a)  Fan assisted natural ventilation (either through a facade, atrium system or roof).
[Ss_65_40_33_56]
b)  Local ventilation supply and/or extract fans.  [Ss_65_40_33_48]
c) Specialist Local Exhaust Ventilation. [Ss_65_40]
d)  Anti-stratification fans.  [Ss_65_40]
4.4.14.2 Any fans provided shall be energy efficient and shall comply with AD L.
