10.7. Thermal Comfort for Special Education Needs
10.7.1 For comfort conditions for people with special requirements such as those with
physical disabilities, BS EN ISO 7730 refers to BS EN ISO/TR 14415:2005. [PM_10_20_90]
10.7.2 Where pupils have special needs that affect their temperature response, or for very
young pupils,  an assessment of their particular needs is required which may mean that
higher categories of comfort criteria are needed in particular areas of a School or across a
whole School. [ PM_35_10_13]
