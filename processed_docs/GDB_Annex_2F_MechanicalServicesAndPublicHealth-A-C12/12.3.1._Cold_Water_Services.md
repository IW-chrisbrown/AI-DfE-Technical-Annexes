## 12.3.1. Cold Water Services
12.3.1.1 A potable/non-potable cold water service system shall be designed, supplied,
installed, tested and commissioned, such that it is capable of providing an adequate supply
of potable and/or non-potable water to draw off points throughout the Building.
[Ss_55_70_38_15]
12.3.1.2 The system shall be designed to a maximum velocity of water in pipework of
2m/sec.  [Ss_55_70_38_15]
12.3.1.3  The system shall be designed to a maximum water temperature in
accordance with HSE L8 (Legionnaires' disease. The control of legionella bacteria in water
systems - Approved Code of Practice and guidance). [  PM_10_20_90]
