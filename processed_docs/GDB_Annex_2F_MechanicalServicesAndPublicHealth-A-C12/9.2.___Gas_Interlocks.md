## 9.2.   Gas Interlocks
9.2.1 An interlock is required between the gas supply and mechanical ventilation to ensure
that gas is not supplied when there is inadequate airflow. This is for safe operation of
appliances and equipment and the safety of personnel. [PM_10_20_82]
