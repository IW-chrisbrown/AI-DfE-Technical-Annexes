## 7.8.2. Infection Control
7.8.2.1 Where there are students with complex health needs, ventilation systems shall be
designed for infection control and to maintain standards of hygiene. Staff shall be able to
control ventilation for comfort, and draughts shall be minimised so as not to affect vulnerable
and immobile students. Legionella is a higher risk to people with complex health needs,
especially the immuno-compromised. Particular attention needs to be paid to legionella
prevention in domestic hot water systems in these schools. [ PM_35_50]
7.8.2.2 Where mechanical ventilation is specified, filtration shall be provided, depending on
external air quality and design exposure levels. [ PM_35_70_94]
