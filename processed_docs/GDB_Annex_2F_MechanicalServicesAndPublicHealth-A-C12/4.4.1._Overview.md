## 4.4.1. Overview
4.4.1.1 In any space, ventilation shall be provided by natural ventilation, by a mixed-mode or
hybrid system, or by mechanical ventilation.  The following hierarchy shall be in place for
ventilation design:
a)  Natural ventilation [PM_35_70_94]
b)  Natural ventilation with fan assistance (hybrid) [PM_35_70_94]
c) Mechanical ventilation [PM_35_70_94]
