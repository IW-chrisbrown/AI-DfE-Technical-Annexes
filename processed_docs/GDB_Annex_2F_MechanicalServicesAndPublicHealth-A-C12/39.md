## 39
a)  Maximum surface temperatures comply with Section 4.3.14. [PM_10_20_90]
b)  The floor covering is appropriate for underfloor heating to allow effective heat transfer
to the room. [  PM_10_20_82]
c) The design and installation are fully coordinated with other underfloor services such as
drainage and electrical cables, and any fixed FF&E. [  PM_10_20_82]
d)  Actuators and manifolds are located in a dedicated cupboard or robust boxing
adjacent to the space being heated but inaccessible to students and the public.
