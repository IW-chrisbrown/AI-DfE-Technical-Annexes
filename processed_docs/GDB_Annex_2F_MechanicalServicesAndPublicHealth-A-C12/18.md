3.3.   Domestic Hot and Cold Water Systems
3.3.1 Domestic hot and cold water shall be provided to toilets, changing rooms and
showers.  [Ss_55_70_38]
3.3.2 Domestic hot and cold water shall be provided to kitchens and food rooms and
cleaners’ stores that have sinks.  [Ss_55_70_38]
3.3.3    Hot water shall only be provided to sinks in Teaching Spaces where there is a clear
need such as in art or food rooms.    [PM_10_20_82]
