## [PM_35_70_36]
4.3.3. Heat Generating Systems and Main Plant
4.3.3.1 The DfE’s preferred methods of heat generating systems are (in order of
preference):
a)  Connection to an available existing or planned community/district heat distribution
network. [  Ss_60_40_37_22]
b)  Ground source heat pumps. [  Ss_60_40_36_35]
c) Air source heat pumps. [  Ss_60_40_36_05]
d)  Other Low or Zero Carbon (LZC) technologies. [  PM_35_70]
e)  Gas fired condensing boilers where all other options have been ruled out and only with
the agreement of the Employer. [  Pr_60_60_08_34]
