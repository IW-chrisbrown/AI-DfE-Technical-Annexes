## 3. [PM_10_20_90]
7.7.5.10 If a recirculatory fume cupboard is used in a science prep room, it shall have a
vertical upwards discharge and the room shall have extract from high level to minimise
pollutants in the occupied zone. [Ss_65_40_32_32]
7.7.5.11 A risk assessment to HSG 258 is required for all fume cupboard installations.
