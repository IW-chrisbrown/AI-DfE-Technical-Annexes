11.3.15 Obstructions to the flow of air (e.g.,  deep external sills and recesses) shall be
taken into account, as these reduce the airflow through the opening. [PM_35_70_94]
11.3.16 Examples of obstructions include cills, recesses and blinds. They can be seen
as another airflow obstruction coefficient, and their presence means their impact on the PPO
free area should be accounted for to achieve the required effective area. [ PM_35_70_94]
