## 4.4.19. Reference Standards
4.4.19.1 The design and installation of any ventilation systems provided shall comply
with the relevant parts of the following standards as listed below, and any updated versions
of these standards. [  PM_10_20_90]
a)  CIBSE Guide B - ‘Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration’.  [FI_70]
b)  ASHRAE 62.1:2013 - ‘The Standards for Ventilation and Indoor Air Quality’.
