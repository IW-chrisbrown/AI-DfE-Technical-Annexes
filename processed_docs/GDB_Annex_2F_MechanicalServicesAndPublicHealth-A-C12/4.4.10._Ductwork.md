## 4.4.10. Ductwork
4.4.10.1 Ductwork velocities shall be in line with CIBSE Guide B. [PM_10_20_90]
4.4.10.2 Ductwork shall be installed in line with BESA DW144. [  PM_10_20_90]
4.4.10.3 Where ductwork is installed in ceiling voids it shall be fully accessible for
maintenance. [PM_80_10_50]
4.4.10.4 Access points for cleaning of ductwork shall be provided. [PM_80_10_50]
4.4.10.5 Where ductwork is exposed, any insulation shall be protected in a robust
cleanable material e.g.,  aluminium or plastic. Self  -finish aluminium foil that can be easily
damaged is not robust enough, but rigid plastic or aluminium sheeting is acceptable.
