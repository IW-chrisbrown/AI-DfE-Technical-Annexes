## 12.2.5. Access Points
12.2.5.1 Rodding and access points shall be provided at all changes of direction to
enable the whole system to be maintained. [PM_80_10_50]
12.2.5.2 Access points shall be provided at the foot of all soil and ventilation pipes, local
access points and horizontal anti-si phon pipes above fitment flood level. [  PM_10_20_82]
12.2.5.3 Access/rodding points shall be located externally or in unoccupied spaces
such as cleaners’  stores. [  PM_10_20_82]
