## 4.3.7. Ground Source Heat Pumps
4.3.7.1 Where Ground Source Heat Pumps are installed, the ecology and environmental
factors shall be appropriate for the installation of such systems.
a)  Closed loop systems shall be used. Justification and approval is required to use an
open loop system. [  Ss_60_40_36_35]
b)  The circulating fluid shall be treated to protect from freezing and to prevent corrosion.
[Ss_60_40_36_35]
c) Vertical ground heat exchangers provide increased efficiency and lower land
requirement than horizontal ground loops and should be considered ahead of
horizontal configuration. [Ss_60_40_36_35]
d)  Careful consideration shall be given to the detailed design of the ground heat
exchanger including issues of ground recharge requirements, annual heat extraction
rates, field depletion and frost heave. [Ss_60_40_36_35]
