## 7.3.1. Design Criteria
7.3.1.1 The following section provides a minimum specification for the design and
installation of the ventilation and associated systems in any food rooms for Secondary
pupils. [PM_35_70_94]
7.3.1.2 There shall be a door between the food room and any linked space such as a food
preparation room to prevent contamination from dust. [PM_10_20_82]
7.3.1.3 Exhaust ventilation rates shall be calculated taking account of room size and usage.
