## 116
11.2.5 Designs shall provide sufficient openable areas in suitable locations for winter, mid-
season and summer conditions; and means by which the occupants can control the
openable areas shall be provided. [PM_40_20_94]
11.2.6 The designer should consider the results of the overheating analysis, which may
show that higher airflow rates are required for either daytime or night-time cooling at certain
times of the year.  [PM_40_20_94]
