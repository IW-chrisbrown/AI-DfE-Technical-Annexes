8.4 Mechanical ventilation
Where hybrid ventilation is being considered, the mechanical ventilation element
needs to be modelled correctly. If it is supply and extract ventilation then a fixed or
demand controlled ventilation rate of outside air can be incorporated in the model. If
the system is extract only with openable windows, the model should be set up with a
zone exhaust and not an exchange rate to outside. It should be noted that for
thermal modelling and overheating assessment purposes mechanical ventilation is
classified as ‘free-running’ in the absence of mechanical cooling and tight
temperature control.
