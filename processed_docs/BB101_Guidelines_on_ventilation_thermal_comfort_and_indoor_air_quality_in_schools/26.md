## 26

Local extract is required from processes or rooms where water vapour or pollutants
are released through activities such as showering, cooking or chemical experiments.
Extract ventilation may be intermittent or continuous.
Local extract to the outside should be provided in sanitary accommodation,
washrooms and food and beverage preparation areas.
Printers and photocopiers used frequently or continuously, may need to be isolated
(to avoid any pollutants entering the occupied space) and local extract should be
provided.
