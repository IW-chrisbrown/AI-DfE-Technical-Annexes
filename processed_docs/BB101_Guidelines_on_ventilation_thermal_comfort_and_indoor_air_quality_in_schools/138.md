Annex A: Carbon dioxide levels in schools
Outside CO2 concentrations are generally around 400 ppm. For a typical classroom
with 30 students and 2 staff, an outdoor air ventilation rate of between 8 and
9 l/s/person corresponds to a carbon dioxide level of around 1000 ppm under steady
state conditions depending on the ventilation system. See calculation method given
in CIBSE Guide A.
