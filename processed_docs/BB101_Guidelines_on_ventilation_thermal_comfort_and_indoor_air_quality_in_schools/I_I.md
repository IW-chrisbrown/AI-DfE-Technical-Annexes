needs must be made. Adaptive comfort thresholds may not be applicable and fixed
temperature thresholds may need to be used. This category applies only to
Designated Units or Special Schools for non-ambulant pupils or those with medical
conditions. Local portable heating and cooling may need to be provided for SEN
students with temperature sensitivities who are educated in mainstream
accommodation.
