7.6 Performance standards for the avoidance of
overheating
Overheating in classrooms and over-glazed larger spaces such as libraries and
learning resource centres frequently occurs. This problem is often reported in post
occupancy and staff surveys. DfE has adopted the adaptive thermal comfort method
from BS EN 15251 to deal with this problem.
