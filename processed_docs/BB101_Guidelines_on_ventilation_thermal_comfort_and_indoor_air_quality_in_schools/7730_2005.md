## 7730: 2005
An acceptable moderate level of expectation
IV Low level of expectation. This category should only
be accepted for a limited part of the year

BS EN 15251 gives comfort criteria for both mechanically cooled buildings and for
free running buildings. A free running building is defined as a building, with either
natural or mechanical ventilation, which is not actively heated or cooled.
For refurbished buildings, the minimum standard is Category IV where Category III
cannot be met for reasons of practicality and due to the extent of refurbishment.
However, after refurbishment the criteria should not be worse than before
refurbishment in any aspect affecting thermal comfort.
