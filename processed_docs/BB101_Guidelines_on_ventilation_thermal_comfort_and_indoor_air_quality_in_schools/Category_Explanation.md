## Category Explanation
I  Equivalent to Category
A of EN ISO 7730: 2005
High level of expectation. Also recommended for
spaces occupied by very sensitive and fragile
persons with special requirements like some
disabilities, sick, very young children and elderly
persons, to increase accessibility
II Equivalent to Category
B of EN ISO 7730: 2005
Normal expectation
III  Equivalent to
Category C of EN ISO
